About

A 100-level History student at the University of Ibadan, and the founder of Tagalong Co. — built from a phone, with no starting budget, and a belief that you don't need everything figured out before you begin.

Benjamin studies West African history, archaeology, and the African diaspora — trying to understand the forces that shaped the people who came before him. Alongside that, he's building Tagalong Co., an online store with one message: Small Upgrades, Better Days.

He trains as a competitive swimmer most evenings, and posts about the real version of building a business in Nigeria — the KYC delays and the wins alike — rather than a highlight reel.
